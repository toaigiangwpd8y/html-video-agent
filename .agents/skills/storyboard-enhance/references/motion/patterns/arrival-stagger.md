# Arrival and stagger

**Trigger.** Match when multiple meaningful elements should enter in a readable sequence, or when a new element must be perceived as joining an established composition.

**Compositional purpose.** A useful renderer-neutral shape is to establish the anchor, then bring related elements in a reading order with controlled overlap between arrivals. Let the overlap make the group feel coordinated while each landing remains perceptible. Order and spacing establish priority, grouping, or relationship; every arrival should add meaning or clarify the composition. Preserve an immediate semantic anchor when the opening-frame contract requires one.

**Failure checks.** Check that stagger order follows the idea's hierarchy or causal order; no required element waits until it is needed for comprehension; arrivals do not imply unsupported sequence; and the motion does not turn decorative repetition into false emphasis.
