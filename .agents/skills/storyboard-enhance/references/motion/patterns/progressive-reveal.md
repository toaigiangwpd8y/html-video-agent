# Reveal and progressive disclosure

**Trigger.** Match when information, detail, or structure becomes knowable in meaningful stages rather than all at once.

**Compositional purpose.** Establish the current gist, then disclose each meaningful item when the evidence, narration, or approved discovery makes it relevant. The sequence should keep adding information across the beat; a new item can begin arriving as the prior item settles when both remain readable. Preserve enough prior context for each detail to be understood, and leave the final state available for inspection.

**Failure checks.** Check that no reveal withholds information needed to understand the current claim; each stage adds meaning instead of delay; the final state is not exposed prematurely; and masking or cropping does not misrepresent the underlying evidence.
