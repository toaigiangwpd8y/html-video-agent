# Reposition, settle, and hold

**Trigger.** Match when an established composition changes position, scale, or hierarchy and the viewer needs time to inspect the new state.

**Compositional purpose.** Use a motivated nudge-curve shape: gradually start the established group, make its travel clear as it gives way to a new hierarchy, then gradually settle. Hold the new composition long enough to inspect relevant evidence or copy. A hold is an intentional inspection interval, not wasted time; the movement should resolve a spatial need rather than add ambient activity.

**Failure checks.** Check that the new position changes or clarifies meaning; the move does not hide evidence or break continuity; the settle does not overshoot into distraction; and the hold covers the actual comprehension need without freezing before later narrated meaning has arrived.
